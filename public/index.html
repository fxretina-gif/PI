How the wedding happensPreparation, ceremony & registration
  1. Document & eligibility check

    The couple and all four witnesses attend with their documents. Confirm the final paperwork and ritual arrangements with the mandir.

  2. The Vedic ceremony

    The priest guides the couple through the havan, Vedic mantras, saptapadi and marriage vows.

  3. Marriage records & registration

    Complete the mandir’s records and collect any ceremony certificate issued. Government marriage registration is a separate step through the Marriage Registrar/Sub-Registrar.

Eligibility basics: Bride 18+, groom 21+, both freely consenting and neither already married. The mandir must also check the other applicable legal conditions.

Mandir’s ceremony guide Hindu Marriage Act, §§ 5–8 UP marriage registration

Ceremony items and document quantities follow the organiser’s checklist. Confirm any additional paperwork with the mandir before the wedding.
